# Introduction

FlexStore is an e-commerce platform that gives you everything you need to run an online store. Products, orders, shipping, payments, and the look of your storefront are all handled from one admin panel.

## What You Can Do

- **Manage your catalog** with variants, images, rich descriptions, and inventory tracking
- **Organize products** into hierarchical categories and brand pages
- **Publish a blog** with a rich text editor, scheduling, and homepage placement
- **Process orders**, including refunds, invoices, and a full activity timeline
- **Accept payments** through built-in gateways covering cards, wallets, and local methods, or cash on delivery
- **Configure shipping** with per-region rates by weight or price, or live rates and prepaid labels from a connected carrier
- **Handle taxes** per region, with compound rates and tax categories
- **Run promotions** using coupon codes and time-limited flash sales
- **Track inventory** with stock adjustments, reason logging, and movement history
- **Moderate reviews** submitted by customers
- **Customize your storefront**: homepage sections, menus, announcements, theme colors, and more
- **Support multiple languages and currencies**
- **View reports** on sales, orders, products, returns, customers, and marketing
- **Manage your team** with staff accounts, custom roles, and granular permissions
- **Publish store policies** such as privacy, refunds, and terms of service

## Navigating the Admin Panel

The sidebar is split into two groups. The main group covers day-to-day operations:

- **Dashboard**: key store metrics and analytics
- **Orders**: view, create, and manage orders
    - **Returns**: handle merchandise coming back
    - **Abandoned checkouts**: recover checkouts customers didn't finish
- **Products**: your catalog
    - **Inventory**: stock levels and adjustments
- **Categories**: hierarchical product organization
- **Brands**: product brands
- **Blog posts**: articles for your storefront
- **Coupons**: discount codes
- **Flash sales**: time-limited promotions
- **Customers**: accounts, addresses, and order history
- **Reviews**: moderate product reviews
- **Reports**: sales, order, product, customer, and marketing analytics

The **System** group covers store-wide configuration:

- **Users**: staff accounts
- **Roles**: roles and their permissions
- **Regions**: country and state groups used by shipping, tax, and payment rules
- **Storefront**: homepage sections, menus, announcements, footer, and theme
- **Settings**: store details, shipping, taxes, payments, currencies, languages, newsletters, notifications, SEO, policies, integrations, and system options

Items only appear if your role has permission to access them, so your sidebar may be shorter than this list.

## Your Customers' Experience

Customers can:

- Browse and search your catalog, filtering by category, brand, price, and rating
- View product pages with image galleries, variant selection, and reviews
- Add items to a cart and apply coupon codes
- Check out as a guest or with an account
- Hand the payment to someone else: instead of paying, a shopper can generate a link that lets a friend or family member pay for the checkout. The order is only placed once that payment succeeds.
- Track orders, manage addresses, and keep a wishlist
- Compare products side by side
- Share a cart, wishlist, or comparison by link.
- Submit reviews and ratings
- Subscribe to your newsletter
- Switch languages and currencies
