# Staff

Staff is where you bring team members into your admin panel. Everyone gets their own account with a name, email, and password, and what they can actually do comes from the [roles](/roles) you assign.

## Viewing the Staff List

Go to **Staff** in the System group of the sidebar. Each row shows the name, email, assigned roles, and last login. Sort by name or last login, and filter by role to find someone quickly.

The list covers staff accounts only. Your Super Admin account and customer accounts aren't included.

## Adding a New Staff Member

1. Click **Add staff** in the top-right corner.
2. Fill in the **Full name**. The **URL handle** fills in from the name and forms the address of the person's public author page if they write blog posts. Edit it if you want something different, and keep it unique.
3. Fill in the **Email address** and set an initial **Password**.
4. Check one or more roles in the **Roles** panel on the right.
5. Click **Add staff**.

Check **Add more** before saving to keep the form open for the next person.

## Editing a Staff Member

Click a row to open their edit page, where you can change the name, URL handle, email, and roles. Changing the URL handle changes the address of their author page, so existing links to it stop working. Enter a **New password** to change it, or leave that field blank to keep the current one. Click **Update staff** to save.

## Deleting Staff

Select staff with the checkboxes and click **Delete**, then confirm. Deletion is permanent.

::: tip Securing your own account
Two-factor authentication and passkeys are set up per person on their own [Account > Security](/account#security) page, not from the Staff list.
:::
