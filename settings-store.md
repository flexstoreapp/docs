# Store Settings

The core identity of your store, under **Settings > Store**.

## Store Information

- **Store name**: what customers see throughout your storefront, in the browser tab, and in emails.
- **Store description**: a short line explaining what you sell, shown in the storefront footer. Switch the admin language to enter a translation for each language.
- **Store email**: your main contact address, which may also appear in order confirmations and other customer emails.
- **Store phone**: your main number for customer enquiries.

## Store Address

Your physical address, used for tax calculations and possibly in customer-facing communications. The fields are street address, city, state, postal code, and country.

The form adapts to the country you pick. Labels change to local terminology ("State" and "ZIP code" for the United States, "District" for Bangladesh), countries with a fixed list of subdivisions get a searchable dropdown, and fields a country doesn't use, like a postal code where none exists, disappear.

## Countries You Sell To

The countries you accept orders from. Click **Browse countries** to pick them, and click the × on a tag to remove one.

This list drives every place a country gets chosen: checkout and account address forms, customer and order address forms in the admin panel, and the country code dropdown on phone fields. Addresses in any other country are rejected when the form is saved. Leave the list empty, the default, to sell everywhere.

Four places deliberately keep the full country list, because they aren't about where you sell: your own store address, the default country of origin in shipping settings, a product's country of origin, and the countries covered by a shipping region.

::: tip
Restricting this list is the fastest way to stop orders you can't ship. It doesn't replace shipping zones, though, so you still need rates for the countries you serve.
:::

## Branding

**Logo**: upload separate logos for light and dark mode so your branding works in both. Without one, the store name is shown instead. The same logos go into transactional emails, with the dark logo used by clients that support email dark mode such as Apple Mail and Outlook.

**Favicon**: the small icon in browser tabs. 32x32 or 64x64 pixels is recommended.

## Social Media Links

Enter the full URL for each profile you want shown. Facebook, Instagram, X (Twitter), TikTok, Pinterest, and YouTube are supported, and only the ones you fill in appear on your storefront.
