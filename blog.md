# Blog

The blog lets you publish articles alongside your products: buying guides, announcements, seasonal tips, anything that helps shoppers. Posts live at `/blog` and can be featured on your homepage.

## Blog Post List

Open **Blog posts** from the sidebar. Each row shows the featured image, author, publish date, and status:

- **Published**: live on your storefront.
- **Scheduled**: published with a future date, hidden until that date arrives.
- **Draft**: not visible to shoppers.

Sort by clicking the **Title**, **Publish date**, or **Status** headers. Click **Filters** to search by title or filter by status.

Row checkboxes (and the header checkbox for the whole page) reveal a **Delete** button in the action bar.

## Adding and Editing a Post

Click **Add post** in the top-right corner, or click any row to open that post in the editor. Save with **Add post** or **Update post**. Whoever creates a post is recorded as its author and shown on the post page.

### Post Fields

- **Title**: required, shown in listings and at the top of the post.
- **Excerpt**: a short summary for listing cards and your homepage, up to 500 characters. Leave it blank and cards show the title alone.
- **Content**: the article body in the rich text editor, plus an HTML source view when you need full control.
- **Featured image**: shown on listing cards, at the top of the post, and in social previews. Worth adding to every post.

**Publishing**

- **Status**: **Draft** to keep it hidden, **Published** to make it live.
- **Publish date**: leave it empty on a published post to go live immediately, or set a future date to schedule it. The date also orders your posts, newest first.

**SEO**

- **SEO title**: auto-filled from the post title, up to 70 characters.
- **SEO description**: auto-filled from the excerpt, up to 160 characters.
- **URL handle**: the post's address, such as `choosing-the-right-headphones`. Generated from the title and editable. Use lowercase letters and numbers only, with single hyphens between words: no spaces, capitals, punctuation, or a hyphen at the start or end.

## Deleting Posts

Check the posts you want gone, click **Delete** in the action bar, and confirm. Deletion is permanent. A featured image used only by a deleted post is removed too; images used elsewhere stay.

## How the Blog Appears on Your Storefront

Published posts show up in four places:

- **Blog page** at `/blog`: a grid of post cards, newest first, paginated. Each card shows the featured image, publish date, author, title, and excerpt.
- **Post page**: featured image, title, publish date, author, and full content, followed by other recent posts. The author name links to their author page.
- **Author page**: everything one writer has published, at an address built from that person's URL handle (set on their [staff](/staff) record). Writers with no published posts don't get a page.
- **Homepage**: add a **Blog Posts** section in the Storefront Builder and it pulls in real posts automatically.

Posts per page, how more posts load, and whether related posts appear are all configured under **Blog list** and **Blog detail** in the [Storefront Builder](/storefront). Drafts and scheduled posts stay hidden everywhere until they go live.

## Measuring Readership

Every time a visitor opens a post, FlexStore records a view, counted once per visitor session per post so refreshing doesn't inflate it.

Open **Reports > Blog post views** under Marketing to see total views, unique readers, and which posts are read most over any period. See [Reports](/reports).
