# Policy Settings

Where you write the legal documents for your store, under **Settings > Policy**. Each policy gets its own rich text editor for formatting the document.

## Refund Policy

Your refund and return policy tells customers what happens when they want to send something back. It's one of the biggest trust signals on a store, so it's worth writing carefully.

Worth covering: how long customers have to request a return, what condition items need to be in, whether you offer exchanges, how refunds are processed and how long they take, and anything that can't be returned at all, such as digital downloads or perishables.

## Customer Returns

This page also controls the self-service [Returns](/returns) workflow:

- **Customer returns**: let customers request returns for delivered items from their account. With it off, only your staff can create returns.
- **Return window**: how many days after delivery a customer has to request one. `0` means no time limit.
- **Return instructions**: optional guidance shown when a return is approved, such as where to send items.
- **Restocking fee (%)**: a default deduction from the refund. `0` means none. Staff can override it per return and adjust it again on the refund screen.

See the [Returns](/returns) guide for the whole workflow.

## Privacy Policy

Your privacy policy describes how you collect, use, and store customer data. It's a legal requirement in many countries, particularly once you handle names, email addresses, or payment details.

Worth covering: what personal data you collect and why, how you use it (fulfilling orders, sending marketing), whether you share it with third parties like carriers and payment processors, how customers can have their data deleted or corrected, and what cookies your site uses.

## Terms of Service

Your terms set the rules of the relationship between you and your customers.

Worth covering: how orders are placed and accepted, your pricing and payment terms, limitations of liability, how disputes are handled, and any age restrictions.

Click **Save changes** at the bottom to publish. The policies then appear on your storefront.
