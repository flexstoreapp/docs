# Reviews

Reviews lets you search, filter, moderate, and edit every review left on your store, and control how they appear on product pages.

## The Reviews List

Open **Reviews** from the sidebar. Each row shows the star rating, review title and body, the product, the customer's name and email, the status, and the submission date. Longer lists are paginated.

Search by title or content, and use the filter bar to narrow by product (via the product picker), status, or a specific 1 to 5 star rating. Click the **Rating** or **Date** headers to sort.

## Review Statuses

- **Pending**: submitted but not yet acted on, and hidden from shoppers.
- **Approved**: visible on the product page.
- **Rejected**: declined and hidden from shoppers.

## Approving and Rejecting Reviews

Select rows with the checkboxes on the left and an action bar appears with **Approve**, **Reject**, and **Delete**. All three ask for confirmation, and deletion is permanent.

## Editing a Review

Click a row to open the edit dialog, where you can change the rating (1 to 5), the optional title, and the review text (up to 5,000 characters). Click **Update review** to save.

## Adding a Review Manually

Useful when importing reviews from another platform. Click **Add review** at the top of the page, pick the product and the customer it's attributed to, then set the rating, optional title, and text.

Manually added reviews start as Pending and need approving before they show on the storefront.

## Auto-Approve Setting

New reviews are held as Pending by default. To publish them straight away, go to **Settings > General** and check **Auto-approve reviews**. Uncheck it whenever you want to go back to manual moderation.

## How Reviews Appear on Product Pages

Approved reviews sit in a **Reviews** section below the product description, with an overall star rating and review count, a distribution bar breaking ratings down by star level, and individual cards showing the reviewer's name, date, rating, title, and text. A **Load more** button appears when there are more reviews than the configured page size.

Only logged-in customers who bought the product can submit a review, and each customer gets one review per product. Everyone else simply doesn't see the form.

To turn the section on or off, or change how many reviews load at a time (5, 10, 15, or 20), go to **Storefront > Product detail** in the Storefront Builder.
