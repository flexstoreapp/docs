# Categories

Categories organize your products so customers can browse and find things. You can nest them as deeply as you like: a top-level "Clothing" with "Men's", "Women's", and "Kids'" beneath it, each with their own subcategories.

Open **Categories** from the sidebar.

## Adding a Category

Click **Add category** in the top-right corner. The dialog has two tabs.

**General**

- **Category name**: what customers see on your storefront.
- **Description**: optional, and may appear on the storefront category page.
- **Google product category**: optional. Search Google's product taxonomy and pick the closest match. Products in this category, and in subcategories that do not set their own value, use it in Google Shopping and Meta catalogs.
- **Active**: uncheck to hide the category from customers without deleting it.

**SEO**

- **URL handle**: the address used in the category URL, such as `mens-clothing`. Generated from the name and editable. Use lowercase letters and numbers only, with single hyphens between words: no spaces, capitals, punctuation, or a hyphen at the start or end.
- **SEO title**: the title in search results and the browser tab, auto-filled from the name, up to 70 characters.
- **SEO description**: the summary under the title in search results, auto-filled from the description, up to 160 characters.

Click **Add category** to save. An empty category list offers the same form from its empty state.

## Editing a Category

Hover the category and click the pencil icon on the right. Update anything across the General and SEO tabs and click **Update category**. The dialog shows when the category was last changed.

## Hierarchical Categories

Subcategories appear indented under their parent with connecting lines. To add one, hover the parent, click the plus icon on the right, and fill in the dialog. The parent is set for you.

## Reordering Categories

Category order controls how they display on your storefront. Hover a category, grab the grip handle on the left, and drag it into position. An indicator line shows where it will land. Dropping a category directly onto another makes it a subcategory of that one.

## Searching Categories

The search box near the top filters by category name or URL handle across the whole tree. Matching categories are shown along with their parents so the hierarchy stays readable.

## Deleting a Category

Hover the category, click the trash icon, and confirm.

::: warning
Deleting a category also deletes every subcategory nested beneath it. This cannot be undone.
:::

## Active and Inactive Categories

An inactive category is hidden from your storefront but kept in the admin panel with an **Inactive** badge. This is useful while you prepare a new part of your catalog that isn't ready to go public.
