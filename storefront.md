# Storefront Builder

The Storefront Builder controls how your store looks. Every change shows up in the live preview pane on the right, so you always see what customers will see. Open it by clicking **Storefront** in the admin navigation.

## Homepage Sections

Your homepage is built from sections: modular content blocks you can add, reorder, hide, or remove.

**Adding one**: open **Homepage**, click **Add section**, pick a type from the picker, then give it a title and fill in its settings. New sections land at the bottom of the page.

**Reordering**: drag a section by the grip handle on its left. The order saves automatically.

**Hiding**: each section has a **Show on storefront** toggle. Hiding keeps every setting intact, which is handy for preparing seasonal content in advance.

**Editing**: click a section to open its settings form. The preview refreshes when you save.

**Deleting**: use the row actions and confirm. Deletion is permanent.

## Choosing Products for a Section

Several section types pull from your catalog and share the same **Product source** setting:

- **Latest products**: your most recently added products.
- **Hand-picked products**: chosen one by one with the product picker.
- **Products from a category**: pick a category and the section fills itself.
- **Products from a brand**: same, by brand.
- **Recently viewed products**: personalized per visitor.

Every source except hand-picked has a **Number of products** slider, up to 50 in most sections and up to 20 in Product columns.

## Available Section Types

Every section has a **Title** and a **Show on storefront** toggle on top of the settings below.

### Hero Slider

The large banner at the top of the homepage: a rotating slider with optional side tiles.

Up to 5 slides, each with a background image, headline and subtext, button text and URL, a text color (dark or light), and a text alignment (any of nine positions).

Up to 2 side tiles sit beside the slider, each with an image, title, subtitle, link URL, text color, and alignment.

Slider settings cover autoplay and its speed (1000 to 15000 ms), the animation (Fade or Slide), and whether navigation dots show.

### Info Strip

A row of up to 4 selling points, each with an icon, title, and subtitle. Good for things like free shipping or easy returns.

### Category Grid

A visual grid of up to 12 categories. Each tile takes its own image, with the category name shown over it.

### Featured Products

A product grid using the shared **Product source** settings.

### Deal of the Day

A limited-time offer block with a subtitle, an end date and time, an optional countdown timer, and product source settings.

### Product Tabs

A tabbed product browser so customers can switch views without leaving the homepage. Up to 5 tabs, each with its own label and product source settings.

### Promo Banners

Up to 2 image banners side by side, each with an image, title, subtitle, link URL, text alignment, and text color.

### Product Carousel

A horizontally scrolling product carousel using the shared **Product source** settings.

### Product Columns

Up to 3 side-by-side product lists, each with its own heading and product source settings (20 products max per column).

### Brand Strip

A strip of up to 20 brand logos, optionally shown in black and white via **Grayscale logos**.

### Testimonials

Up to 6 customer quotes you enter yourself, each with the quote, an author name, and a 1 to 5 star rating.

### Blog Posts

A row of article cards from your [blog](/blog). Each card shows the featured image, publish date, author, title, and excerpt, and a "View all posts" link closes the row.

- **Post source**: **Latest posts**, **Hand-picked posts**, or **Posts by an author**.
- **Author**: whose posts fill the section, for the Posts by an author source.
- **Number of posts**: 1 to 12, for every source except hand-picked.
- **Posts**: the specific posts and their order, for the hand-picked source.

Drafts and scheduled posts never appear, and the section hides itself when nothing is published.

### Newsletter

An email signup block with a subtitle and button text.

## Header

Click **Header** in the sidebar.

### Header Options

- **Sticky header**: keeps the header visible while customers scroll.
- **Language switcher**: shows a language selector, for multilingual stores.
- **Currency switcher**: shows a currency selector.

### Browse Categories

The header carries a **Browse categories** menu. Add the categories you want listed and each one shows its subcategories automatically. Per category you can enable **Show as mega menu** to put the subcategories in a wide dropdown panel, and add a **Featured image**, **Featured title**, and **Featured URL** inside that panel.

### Navigation Menu

Header links can nest up to three levels deep.

1. Click **Add menu item** and enter a label.
2. Choose the link type: **Custom URL**, **Brand**, **Category**, **Flash sale**, or **Page** (Home, Products, Categories, Brands, Blog, Flash sales, Wishlist, Compare, Track order, Refund policy, Privacy policy, Terms of service).
3. Choose whether it opens in the same window or a new tab.
4. Top-level items can enable **Display as mega menu**, showing child links in a wide dropdown with an optional featured image, title, and URL.
5. Use **Show on storefront** to publish or hide the link, then save.

Drag links by the grip handle to reorder. To nest one, drag it slightly right beneath its intended parent.

## Footer

Click **Footer** in the sidebar.

### Footer Elements

Toggle each element on or off:

- **Copyright text**
- **Social media links**, configured in [Store Settings](/settings-store#social-media-links)
- **Payment badges**
- **Powered by** attribution

### Copyright Text

Enter your own notice, such as "All rights reserved." Leave it blank for the default notice with the current year.

### Payment Badges Selection

- **All payment methods**
- **Credit cards only**: Visa, Mastercard, Amex, and other card networks
- **Digital wallets only**: PayPal, Apple Pay, Google Pay, and similar
- **Custom selection**: pick from Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover, JCB, UnionPay, Mada, PayPal, Apple Pay, Google Pay, UPI, and iDEAL

### Footer Links

Click **Add link** and fill in the label and destination. The link types match the header navigation (Custom URL, Brand, Category, Flash sale, Page). Footer links nest one level deep.

## Announcements

Announcement bars are thin banners above the header on every storefront page, useful for promotions, shipping notices, or policy updates. Click **Announcements** in the sidebar.

Click **Add announcement** and fill in:

- **Content**: the message, such as "Free shipping on orders over $50!"
- **URL**: an optional destination
- **Start date & time**: blank means show immediately
- **End date & time**: blank means show indefinitely
- **Show on storefront**: activate or deactivate without deleting

When several announcements are active at once the storefront cycles through them. Drag by the grip handle to set the order. Inactive announcements stay in the list, dimmed, with their settings intact.

## Product List Page

Where customers browse your catalog. Click **Product list** in the sidebar.

- **Loading method**: **Pagination**, **Load more button**, or **Infinite scroll**.
- **Default per page**: 24, 36, 48, or 60. Customers can change this from the page toolbar.
- **Default sort**: Latest, Price low to high, Price high to low, Name A to Z, or Name Z to A.

## Product Detail Page

Click **Product detail** in the sidebar.

### Info Strip

A row of up to 3 icons with titles and subtitles on every product page, good for universal selling points like free shipping, warranty, or secure checkout. Click **Add item** and pick an icon from the library, then write a title ("Free Shipping") and subtitle ("On all orders over $50").

### Reviews

Toggle approved customer reviews on or off, and set **Reviews per page** to 5, 10, 15, or 20.

### Related Products

Toggle the suggestion row at the bottom of each product page, and set **Number of products** to 5, 10, 15, or 20.

Related products are picked by relevance score. Sharing the same **category** is the strongest signal, followed by the same **brand** and a **similar price** (within 30%), with in-stock products first. Only active products qualify. When there aren't enough strong matches, the row is topped up from a broader parent category, then from your newest products as a last resort.

## Blog List Page

Where readers browse published posts at `/blog`. Click **Blog list** in the sidebar. These settings apply to author pages too.

- **Loading method**: **Pagination**, **Load more button**, or **Infinite scroll**.
- **Default per page**: 6, 12, 24, or 48.

## Blog Detail Page

Click **Blog detail** in the sidebar.

### Related Posts

Toggle the row at the bottom of each post and set **Number of related posts** to 3, 6, 9, or 12. Related posts are simply your most recent published posts, excluding the one being read.

## Theme

Click **Theme** in the sidebar to pick your accent color, used for buttons, links, highlights, and interactive elements across the storefront. There are 12 options: Blue, Green, Rose, Gold, Teal, Magenta, Brown, Orange, Red, Pink, Terracotta, and Violet.

Click a swatch to apply it and the preview updates immediately. The same color accents your transactional emails, so customer emails stay consistent with your storefront.

## Custom CSS

Click **Custom CSS** to add your own styles. The editor has syntax highlighting, and the styles apply to every page of your storefront. Click **Save changes** and they take effect immediately.

## Custom JavaScript

Click **Custom JavaScript** to add your own scripts, which run on every storefront page. This is the place for third-party analytics, chat widgets, and tracking scripts. Click **Save changes** and they take effect immediately.

> Be careful here. An error in your script can break your store for every visitor, so test thoroughly after any change.
