# Customers

The Customers section covers everyone with an account on your store. Browse and search the list, add accounts by hand, update details, manage saved addresses, and check order history. Open **Customers** from the sidebar.

## Customer List

Each row shows the customer's name and email, how many orders they've placed, their lifetime value, and when they last logged in.

Search by name or email in the bar at the top and the list updates as you type. Click any column header to sort, and again to reverse. Longer lists are paginated.

## Adding a Customer

Useful when you're taking a phone order or registering a walk-in.

1. Click **Add customer** in the top-right corner.
2. Enter the **full name**, a unique **email address**, and a **password** to share with them.
3. Click **Add customer**.

Check **Add more** before saving to clear the form and keep going.

## Editing Customer Details

Click a customer's name to open their profile, where you can change the full name, email, and password. Leave the password blank to keep the current one. Click **Update customer** and a confirmation appears.

## Managing Customer Addresses

Saved addresses appear during checkout and live on the customer's profile, below their basic details in the **Addresses** section. Each one lists the recipient name, phone number, and full address, and the one marked **Default** is pre-selected at checkout.

**To add one**, click **Add address** and fill in the first and last name, street address (plus apartment or suite if needed), city, province or state, postal code, country, and an optional phone number. Check **Set as default** if it should be their primary address, then click **Save**.

**To edit or delete one**, hover the address and use the pencil or trash icon on the right. Deleting is permanent and asks for confirmation first.

## Viewing Order History

The **Total orders** and **Lifetime value** columns give you the quick picture. For the full history, go to **Orders** and search or filter by the customer's name or email.

## Deleting Customers

Deletion is handled through selection, including for a single customer. Check the box next to each customer, or use the header checkbox to take the whole page, then click the **Delete** button above the table and confirm. This is permanent.
