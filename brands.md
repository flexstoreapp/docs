# Brands

Brands link products to the companies or labels that make them, so shoppers can browse everything from one manufacturer on a dedicated page.

## Brand List

Open **Brands** from the sidebar. The list shows every brand with its linked product count and whether it's active.

Sort by clicking the **Brand name** or **Products** headers. Click **Filters** to search by name or to show only active or inactive brands.

Each row has a checkbox, and the header checkbox selects everything on the page. With brands selected, a **Delete** button appears in the action bar.

## Adding and Editing a Brand

Click **Add brand** in the top-right corner to open the dialog, or click any row to open it pre-filled with that brand's details. Save with **Add brand** or **Update brand**.

**General tab**

- **Brand name**: required, and shown to shoppers.
- **Brand description**: optional, shown on the brand's storefront page.
- **Brand logo**: an optional square image used on brand cards.
- **Active**: uncheck to hide the brand from shoppers without deleting it.

**SEO tab**

- **URL handle**: the address of the brand page, such as `nike`. Generated from the name and editable. Use lowercase letters and numbers only, with single hyphens between words: no spaces, capitals, punctuation, or a hyphen at the start or end.
- **SEO title**: the title in search results and the browser tab, auto-filled from the name, up to 70 characters.
- **SEO description**: the summary under the title in search results, auto-filled from the description, up to 160 characters.

## Deleting Brands

Check the brands you want gone, click **Delete** in the action bar, and confirm. Deletion is permanent, and products linked to a deleted brand are simply left without one.

## How Brands Appear on Your Storefront

Active brands show up in two places. The **Brands page** lists every active brand as a grid of logos sorted alphabetically, and each **brand page** lists that brand's active products with the same filtering and sorting as the main product list, under the brand name and description.

Inactive brands and their pages are hidden from shoppers entirely.
