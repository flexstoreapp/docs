# General Settings

Store-wide preferences that don't belong anywhere more specific. Find them under **Settings > General**.

## Default Low Stock Threshold

When a product's available quantity drops to this number or below, it's flagged as low in stock. Products with their own threshold ignore this. Defaults to 10.

## Auto-Approve Reviews

On, and customer reviews publish immediately. Off, and every review waits for your approval before appearing on the storefront. Leave it off if you want to read submissions before customers do.
