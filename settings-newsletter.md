# Newsletter Settings

Connect an email marketing service so subscribers land in your mailing list automatically. Find it under **Settings > Newsletter**.

## Where Customers Subscribe

Customers subscribe through the **Newsletter** section on your homepage, which you add in the [Storefront Builder](/storefront). The form asks for an email address and shows a confirmation once submitted.

## How It Works

You configure each provider separately with its credentials, then activate one. Only one can be active at a time, so switching providers just means activating a different card, which deactivates the previous one.

With no active provider, the subscription form still appears but submissions go nowhere.

## Supported Providers

Click a provider's card and enter its credentials.

**Mailchimp** needs an **API key** (Account > Extras > API keys) and an **Audience ID** for the list new subscribers join, found in your audience settings.

**Mailerlite** needs an **API key** from Integrations > API.

**Kit** (formerly ConvertKit) needs an **API key** from Settings > Developer and a **Form ID**, which appears in the URL when you view a form in your Kit dashboard.

**Beehiiv** needs an **API key** from Settings > Integrations > API and a **Publication ID**, which starts with `pub_` and lives in your publication settings.

## Activating and Deactivating

Use the toggle on a provider's card to make it active. To stop forwarding subscribers, toggle the active provider off. Your credentials are kept either way, so you can re-enable it whenever you like.
