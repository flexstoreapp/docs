# Currency Settings

Define which currency your store uses and, optionally, which others international shoppers can pick. Find it under **Settings > Currency**.

## Base Currency

All product prices are stored and displayed in the base currency unless a customer has chosen another one. The dropdown lists only currencies you've added and marked active below.

::: warning
Changing the base currency does not convert your existing product prices. You'll need to update them by hand.
:::

Click **Save changes** to apply.

## Managing Currencies

The currency list below the settings form is where you add, edit, and remove currencies. Marking one active makes it available on your storefront right away.

### Adding a Currency

Click **Add currency** and provide:

- **Currency**: pick it from the dropdown.
- **Symbol**: pre-filled from your choice, and editable.
- **Exchange rate**: relative to your base currency. With USD as base and EUR at 0.92, a $100 product shows as €92.
- **Symbol position**: before the amount (`$100`), before with a space (`$ 100`), after (`100€`), or after with a space (`100 €`).
- **Thousands separator** and **decimal separator**: the characters used in `1,000` and `10.50`.
- **Decimal places**: 2 for `$10.50`, 0 for `¥100`.
- **Active**: whether customers can pick it in the switcher and use it at checkout.

A live preview below the format fields shows exactly how prices will look.

### Editing and Deleting

Click any row to edit every field. The delete button removes a currency, though the base currency can't be deleted until you switch the base to something else.

### Searching and Sorting

Search by name or code above the list, and click column headers to sort by code, symbol, or exchange rate.

Exchange rates are never updated automatically, so keep them current yourself if you want accurate international pricing.
